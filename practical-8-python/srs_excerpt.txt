SRS excerpt: Account access for the ShopEasy web application

REQ-01: A visitor can create an account using an email address and password.
The email address must not already belong to another account. Passwords must
contain at least 12 characters, including at least one uppercase letter and
one digit. The system must not store a password as plain text.

REQ-02: A newly registered account must verify its email address using a
verification link sent to that address. The link expires after 24 hours.
An account cannot sign in until its email address has been verified.

REQ-03: A registered user can sign in with a verified email address and the
correct password. After five consecutive failed sign-in attempts, the account
is locked for 15 minutes. A successful sign-in resets the failed-attempt
counter.

REQ-04: A user who cannot remember their password can request a password
reset link by email. The reset link expires after 30 minutes and can be used
only once. After a successful reset, the user can sign in using the new
password.
